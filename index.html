3. Commercial Logic

Client Action

Requests manpower → Reviews candidates → Conducts final interview → Selects candidate → Completes employment

Recruitment Co. Action

Accepts requirement → Sources, screens, shortlists → Coordinates submission → Confirms placement → Maintains record / guarantee

Payment Trigger

No fee yet until selection → Fee payable on confirmation → Invoice paid per terms
